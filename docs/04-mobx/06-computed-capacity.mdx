**Setup from step 5:** the temporary safety limit becomes the original chapter's `limit = 4`. The supporting UI now displays capacity and a total, and reads two getter stubs. The starter still guards the numeric limit inside `addCounter`, so it cannot grow without bound while you complete the computed values.

Implement `canAddMoreCounters` and `sum`, then reuse the capacity getter in the action. Both values are derived from existing state. A separate `isFull` field or manually updated total would duplicate facts and could drift out of sync.

Add four counters, give them different values, and reset one. The total must reflect each change, and the disabled Add button must match the store's own guard.
